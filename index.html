Dedupe Pilot

A Google Sheets add-on that finds and merges duplicate contacts.

What Dedupe Pilot does: Dedupe Pilot is a Google Sheets add-on. You open it from Extensions → Dedupe Pilot in a spreadsheet that holds a contact list (for example leads, customers or members, with a header row). It finds rows that are the same person: the same email address, the same phone number with a matching name or company, or names and companies that are spelled differently but match closely. It then writes the results to four new tabs in the same spreadsheet: Clean (one row per contact), Report (which original rows were merged), Review (unclear pairs for you to decide) and Invalid (malformed emails or phone numbers). Your original tab is never edited.

No account or sign-in is needed to read this page. Dedupe Pilot's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are public. Status: awaiting Google Workspace Marketplace approval.

What it catches that exact-match tools miss

  • Same person, different spelling: "Jon Smtih" / "JON SMITH" / "jon smith"
  • Same company, different suffix: "Acme Inc." = "ACME, LLC" = "acme"
  • Same phone, different format: (512) 555-0134 = 512.555.0134 = +1 512 555 0134
  • Shared mailboxes: two different names on one email go to the Review tab, so they aren't merged by accident

Safe by design

  • Your original tab is never edited. Results go to new tabs: Clean, Report, Review, Invalid.
  • Every merged row lists the original row numbers it came from. Nothing silently disappears.
  • Runs inside Google Sheets. It can only access the spreadsheet you open it in, and it sends no spreadsheet data to us or anyone else.

Google permissions it asks for

  • See, edit, create and delete only the spreadsheet it is opened in (spreadsheets.currentonly): to read the contact tab and write the result tabs.
  • Display content in a sidebar or dialog inside Google Sheets (script.container.ui): for the Dedupe Pilot sidebar.
  • Google also lists your email address and basic profile (userinfo.email, userinfo.profile) as default scopes for Marketplace add-ons. Dedupe Pilot does not read or store them.

How accurate is it?

On a test file of 1,111 generated (fake) contact rows with known duplicates, Dedupe Pilot found 99.6% of true duplicate pairs and 98.2% of its merges were correct. Unclear pairs are sent to a Review tab for you to decide. Your results depend on your data; always spot-check the Review tab.
