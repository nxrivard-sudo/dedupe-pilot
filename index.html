What it catches that exact-match tools miss

  • Same person, different spelling: "Jon Smtih" / "JON SMITH" / "jon smith"
  • Same company, different suffix: "Acme Inc." = "ACME, LLC" = "acme"
  • Same phone, different format: (512) 555-0134 = 512.555.0134 = +1 512 555 0134
  • Shared mailboxes: two different names on one email go to a review list, so they aren't merged by accident

Safe by design

  • Your original tab is never edited. Results go to new tabs: Clean, Report, Review, Invalid.
  • Every merged row lists the original row numbers it came from. Nothing silently disappears.
  • Runs inside your spreadsheet. It only has access to the file you open it in, and it sends no data to us or anyone else.

How accurate is it?

On a test file of 1,111 generated (fake) contact rows with known duplicates, Dedupe Pilot found 99.6% of true duplicate pairs and 98.2% of its merges were correct. Unclear pairs are sent to a Review tab for you to decide. Your results depend on your data; always spot-check the Review tab.
