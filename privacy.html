Dedupe Pilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-07 · App: Dedupe Pilot (Google Sheets add-on) · Operator: Dedupe Pilot is operated by Hook & Cook, 615 Avenida Victoria, San Clemente, CA · Contact: nxrivard@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the Dedupe Pilot Google Sheets add-on ("Dedupe Pilot", "we", "us") accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the add-on and to this website.

1. What Dedupe Pilot does

Dedupe Pilot finds duplicate contacts in a Google Sheets tab (same email, same phone with a matching name or company, or closely matching names and companies) and writes the results to new tabs in the same spreadsheet: Clean, Report, Review and Invalid. It never edits your original tab.

3. How the data is used

  • When you click the run button in the sidebar, the add-on reads the values in the active tab, processes them inside Google's Apps Script environment, and writes the result tabs back into the same spreadsheet.
  • The add-on makes no requests to our servers or any third-party server. It has no permission to connect to external services, and none of your spreadsheet data is sent to us or anyone else. (The sidebar loads Google's standard add-on stylesheet from Google's own servers; no spreadsheet data is included in that request.)
  • Your data is used only to provide the de-duplication you asked for. It is not used for advertising, not used to build user profiles, not sold, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • No human at Dedupe Pilot can see your spreadsheet data.

4. What is stored, and where

  • Your spreadsheet data: stays in your spreadsheet. We do not keep a copy.
  • Pro license key: if you enter a Pro key (DP1-…), it is saved in Google's per-user storage for this add-on (Apps Script User Properties) inside your Google account, so Pro stays active. The key is checked inside the add-on without any network call. We cannot read it.
  • We store nothing else from the add-on. There are no cookies or analytics on this website.

5. Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data or spreadsheet data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising. The only exception would be if the law required it, and even then we hold no spreadsheet data to disclose.

6. Purchases (Pro)

Pro purchases are handled by Square, our payment processor (billed as Hook & Cook). Square receives your name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card details. We receive your email address and order details so we can send your license key and provide support. This is separate from the add-on: no Google user data is involved.

7. Retention

  • Spreadsheet data: none retained by us; it never reaches us.
  • Order records (email, order details): kept as long as required for tax and accounting.
  • Support emails: deleted after 24 months.

8. Your choices and deletion

  • Remove the result tabs: delete them in Google Sheets like any other tab.
  • Uninstall: in Google Sheets, Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons, then remove Dedupe Pilot.
  • Revoke access: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select Dedupe Pilot, and remove its access. The add-on then can no longer access any spreadsheet.
  • Order or support data: email nxrivard@gmail.com to ask for a copy or deletion, subject to tax and accounting rules.

9. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Dedupe Pilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide and improve the user-facing de-duplication feature described above; it is not transferred to others, not used for advertising, and not read by humans except with your explicit permission for support, for security reasons, or to comply with applicable law.

10. Children

Dedupe Pilot is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.
