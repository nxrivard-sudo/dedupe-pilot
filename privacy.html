Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-03 · Operator: Dedupe Pilot (operator name TBD) · Contact: support@ (TBD)

What the add-on accesses

Dedupe Pilot asks for two Google permissions: view and manage spreadsheets that this application has been installed in (only the spreadsheet you open it in) and display content in a sidebar/prompt. It cannot open your other files.

How your data is used

When you click "Find & merge duplicates", the add-on reads the active tab, processes it inside Google's Apps Script environment, and writes results to new tabs in the same spreadsheet. Your spreadsheet data is not sent to the developer or any third party, is not stored outside your spreadsheet, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

What we store

If you enter a Pro license key, the key is saved in your Google account's per-user add-on storage (Apps Script User Properties) so Pro stays active. We store nothing else from the add-on.

Purchases

Pro purchases are handled by our checkout provider (Square — our payment processor account, billed as Hook & Cook), which receives your name, email, and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your email and order details to provide your license and support.

Sharing, retention, security

We do not sell or share personal data. Order records are kept as long as required for tax and accounting. Support emails are deleted after 24 months. You can uninstall the add-on at any time (Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons) and delete the stored key by removing the add-on's access in your Google Account settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Dedupe Pilot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
